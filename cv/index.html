Publications

  • 2023
    A Computer Vision-Based Fire Detection and Control System
    Springer — Fire Technology

    Developed a Raspberry Pi-based real-time fire detection system using image processing techniques including histogram analysis, centroid tracking, and variance-based filtering. The system activates a relay-controlled pump and sprinkler setup for automated fire suppression. Achieved 95.26% detection accuracy with a 7.95% improvement in accuracy and 9.43% improvement in precision over existing algorithms.

  • 2025
    Debiasing Natural Language Inference Models Using a Product of Experts Approach
    UT Austin — Research

    Explored the Product of Experts (PoE) debiasing framework to reduce shortcut dependence in NLI models. Used ELECTRA-small as both baseline and main model, with a hypothesis-only classifier as the bias model. Evaluated on SNLI and HANS benchmarks, demonstrating that PoE improves robustness by forcing the model to learn beyond superficial heuristic patterns. Included training dynamics analysis, dataset cartography, and confusion-matrix evaluation.

  • 2025
    Detecting Integrity Events in Airbnb's Sparse Data Streams
    UT Austin — Research

    Proposed a Semantic Neural Hawkes Process (S-NHP) framework that models integrity detection as a self-exciting process over sparse textual signals. Combines temporally contrastive sentence embeddings, interpretable multi-head semantic excitation kernels, and Extreme Value Theory (EVT) based tail modeling. Captures Semantic Temporal Resonance — causally related safety narratives that amplify risk intensity over time. Demonstrated substantial reduction in false positives compared to memoryless baselines while detecting integrity events earlier.
